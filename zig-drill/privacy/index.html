Privacy Policy for Zig Drill

Effective date: 27 September 2026
Developer: Vishnu Surendran Pillai
Contact: vipillainewai99@gmail.com

Zig Drill ("the app") is a single-player game. This policy explains what data the app and the third-party services it uses collect, and why.

1. Data the app itself stores

The app stores your current level, your best depth and whether you have purchased Remove Ads. This is kept only on your device, is not sent to the developer, and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app has no accounts and the developer runs no servers that receive your data.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, operated by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as model, operating system version and app version), IP address and approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data (such as ads viewed and tapped). Google uses this data as described in its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and can change their choice at any time from Privacy options on the main menu.

3. In-app purchases: Google Play Billing

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; the developer never receives your card number or billing address. The app receives only a confirmation that the purchase was made, so it can turn off ads and restore the purchase on reinstall. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact the developer at the address above.

5. Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your personal data and does not share it with anyone other than the service providers named above, which process it to provide ads and purchases.

6. Security and retention

Data stored by the app stays on your device until you uninstall the app. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

7. Your choices

You can stop all data collection by the app by uninstalling it. You can manage ad personalization in your device settings as described in section 2.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date.
