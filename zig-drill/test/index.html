Help test Zig Drill

A one-tap drilling game for Android. Google needs a group of real testers to try it for 14 days before it can go on the Play Store. Thank you for helping!

You need an Android phone or tablet. Do the steps in order, signed in with the same Google account your Play Store uses.

  1. Join the tester group

    Opens Google Groups. Tap Join group.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a minute and try again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install Zig Drill

    Opens the Play Store. Tap Install.

    Install from Google Play

Please keep the game installed for 14 days and play it a few times. If testers leave early, Google restarts the count.
