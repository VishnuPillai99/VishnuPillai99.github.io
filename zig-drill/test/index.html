Help test Zig Drill

A one-tap drilling game for Android. Google needs real testers for 14 days before it can go on the Play Store. Thank you for helping!

Open this page in Chrome first. WhatsApp's built-in browser isn't signed in to Google, so the steps below won't work in it.

Open in Chrome

If nothing happens: tap ⋮ (top right) → Open in Chrome.

This test needs an Android phone or tablet. iPhones can't join, sorry!

Each button opens a Google page. Do what it says, then come back here and the next step lights up.

  1. Join the tester group

    Tap Join group on the Google page, then come back.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Tap Become a tester. If it says the app isn't available, wait a minute and tap it again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install Zig Drill

    Opens the Play Store. Tap Install.

    Install from Google Play

All done, thank you! 🎉 Enjoy the game.

Please keep the game installed for 14 days and play it a few times. If testers leave early, Google restarts the count.
